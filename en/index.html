09 · Before you travel

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer transfers?

Yes. For an extra fee, our partners will gladly arrange a transfer from the railway station or to the ski lifts.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, by prior arrangement. Please message or call us before booking.

How does payment work?

A 30% deposit is required when booking. If you cancel at least 21 days before arrival, we refund the deposit in full. The balance is paid on arrival.

Do you have a baby cot?

Yes, on request.

Are there discounts for military personnel?

Yes, 10% off rooms occupied by service members.

Is the pool open in winter?

Sadly the pool is closed in winter, but the hot jacuzzi is open.
